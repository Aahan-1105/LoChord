# LoChord
I wanted to create a cool synth keypad for music week in the Half-Life Challenge by Hack Club. Given my lack of experience in the realm, I chose to follow along with the MIDI Keypad tutorial by Kai. My project is inspired by the HiChord and aptly named the LoChord.

I wanted to get an ENIG surface for this PCB but unfortunately that was well outside of the budget and would cost an extra $18 USD.

## Goals and Features
- 7 Capacitive Touch Inputs
- 6 Modifier Keys based on the KailH Choc V1 Switch (Hot-swappable)
- A Rotary Encoder for easy volume control
- Swappable ESP32-S3-ZERO
- DIY Cost Under $40 USD!
